Ranking Our Paid Social Destinations: What 8 Launch LP Tests Reveal

How custom LPs have performed against Homepage, PDP, Quiz, Shop All, and Collections on paid traffic · Blushing Stick, Lip Recharge, Mini Miracle Balm, Eyeshadow Stick, Sleepless Nights Kit

TL;DR

The Performance-framed Shopify LP format is emerging as our best-performing LP type. It ranked 1st on TSNK (54K sessions, +5.4% RPS over Homepage), 1st on Blushing Stick Emily (matured), and 2nd on Blushing Stick Branded (matured). Listicle LPs still win at our biggest launch volumes (Eyeshadow Stick, Lip Recharge, both 500K+ sessions). Homepage is the reliable middle-of-the-pack default. PDP, Quiz, Shop All, and generic Collection pages consistently underperform.

Key Findings — Updated

Finding 01 · Revised
Listicle LPs win at launch-scale volume; Homepage wins everything smaller

On our two biggest launches with 500K+ sessions (Eyeshadow Stick, Lip Recharge), Listicle LPs won. On Mini MB (222K sessions) and Blushing Stick (now ~30K combined sessions), Homepage wins. The matured Blushing Stick data reinforces this: as the test matured to 10K+ sessions per arm on Branded, Listicle's ranking actually got worse, not better. Volume thresholds matter more than we initially thought.

Finding 02 · Strengthened
Performance-framed Shopify LPs are now our most consistent top performer

This finding has gone from "emerging signal" to "real pattern" with the TSNK data. Performance LPs have now ranked: 1st on TSNK ($1.57 RPS, +5.4% over Homepage at 54K sessions), 1st on Blushing Stick Emily ($0.72 RPS), and 2nd on Blushing Stick Branded ($0.98 RPS, beating Listicle by 53%). Three top-2 finishes across three tests at meaningful volume. This is the strongest signal in the dataset right now — and the format wasn't even tested on ES Stick or Lip Recharge.

Finding 03
Storytelling-framed LPs consistently underperform Performance-framed LPs

The clearest signal in matured Blushing Stick data: Emily Storytelling ($0.53 RPS) is the worst-performing variant on the Emily test, while Emily Performance ($0.72) is the leading variant. Even within the new Shopify LP format, copy framing matters: lead with claims and product benefits, not narrative. Reinforces the Lip Recharge finding (Problem-First beat Social Proof).

Finding 04
Image-led Listicles beat Headline-led Listicles

ES Stick tested both and Image-led beat Headline-led by ~13% CVR (1.06% vs 0.94%). For Foundation Stick launch, the Listicle variant should lead with a compelling before/after or product-in-use image, not a hook headline.

Finding 05 · Refined
PDP and Shop All still lose; Collections is more nuanced than we thought

PDP and Shop All have never won a test. But TSNK introduced a wrinkle on Collections: the Mother's Day Gifting Collection ($1.47 RPS) was competitive with Homepage ($1.49) — only 1.4% apart. This isn't a generic Collection page — it's a curated seasonal gifting page, so it functions more like a custom LP than a category browse. Doesn't change the rule against generic /collections/ paths, but does suggest curated Collection pages can be viable when the content matches the moment.

Finding 06
Quiz as a paid LP is still not winning, but YouTube data is interesting

MB Quiz came in 3rd of 6 on Mini MB. Blushing Stick Branded/Emily tests didn't include Quiz. But matured Blushing Stick YouTube test now shows Quiz leading ($0.95 RPS) — small sample (406 visitors), but a different signal than other channels. May be worth a YouTube-specific Quiz test post-launch.

Finding 07
TOF audiences are confirmed cold, not broken

Matured TOF data: 1 order across 2,800 visitors (0.04% CVR). ATC rate 8x lower than Branded. This is genuine cold-traffic behavior, not a tracking issue. LP testing on TOF requires much more volume than we typically have.

Open Questions

1. Why did Mini MB Homepage win when Lip Recharge and ES Stick Listicle LPs won?

Hypotheses: (a) MB is JRB's hero product so audience is pre-sold, needs less LP content; (b) Mini MB is a price-tier extension, not a new product, so Homepage browsing makes more sense. Foundation Stick is closer to ES Stick / Lip Recharge (new product, new use case) — which would argue for Listicle.

2. Does the Blushing Stick Branded "Homepage wins" finding actually hold at launch scale?

Blushing Stick has ~2K visitors per arm. ES Stick and Lip Recharge have 150K+. Volume transition may flip the winner. Worth re-testing during Foundation Stick launch with higher spend.

3. Image-led or Problem-First — which Listicle format for Foundation Stick?

Both have evidence. Recommendation: Image-led layout with Problem-First headline. Combine the winning elements from ES Stick (layout) and Lip Recharge (copy framing).

4. Should we test TOF separately for Foundation Stick?

Blushing Stick TOF was underpowered. If Foundation Stick launch gets meaningful TOF spend, rerun a TOF LP test at higher volume. Otherwise route TOF to the same Listicle LP.

Caveats to flag: TSNK is still running (13 days in) and the p2bb on RPS is only 55% — directional, not conclusive. The Performance LP win could narrow with more data. That said, the consistency across TSNK + matured Blushing Stick is what's driving the recommendation shift, not any single test. Lip Recharge and Mini MB tests are also still running — worth re-pulling closer to 5/12. Mother's Day Collection performance on TSNK was likely boosted by the Mother's Day moment specifically; don't generalize that finding to all curated Collection pages.
