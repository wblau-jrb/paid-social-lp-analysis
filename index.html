Paid Social LP Test Analysis · Growth

Ranking Our Paid Social Destinations: What 12 Launch LP Tests Reveal

How custom LPs have performed against Homepage, PDP, Quiz, Shop All, and Collections on paid traffic · Blushing Stick, Lip Recharge, Mini Miracle Balm, Eyeshadow Stick, Sleepless Nights Kit, Foundation Stick

TL;DR

The "one winning LP format" story has weakened as data has matured. Listicle LPs still win at our biggest launch volumes (Eyeshadow Stick, Lip Recharge) and are stable mid-pack performers across most other tests. Homepage is the most reliable middle-of-the-pack default, never finishing first but rarely finishing last. Performance-framed LPs went from "emerging winner" to mixed — they led TSNK early but were overtaken by Mother's Day Collection as the test matured. Foundation Stick funnel-stage testing has produced new patterns: Quiz is winning at MOF, Performance LP looks strong at BOF (sample-limited), and Homepage leads at TOF. PDP, Shop All, and generic Collection pages still underperform.

Key Findings — Updated

Finding 01
Listicle LPs are the most consistent top-2 performers across all launches

Across the dataset, Listicle LPs have finished 1st on Eyeshadow Stick ($0.92 RPS) and Lip Recharge ($0.63), 2nd on Mini MB, Blushing Stick Branded (matured back to 2nd at $1.05), Blushing Stick Emily, and Foundation Stick MOF. They're never dominant winners but they consistently outperform PDP, Shop All, and Collections. Image-led format and Problem-First copy framing have the strongest sub-test signals.

Finding 02 · Revised
Performance-framed LPs are inconsistent across tests and funnel stages

Earlier read had this as our strongest emerging signal. Updated data is more mixed: TSNK Performance LP fell from 1st to 3rd as the test matured to 137K sessions (Mother's Day Collection overtook it). Foundation Stick BOF Performance LP is winning strongly at $1.39 RPS (+77% over the next-best variant), with p2bb 99% on CVR. Foundation Stick MOF Performance LP is in last place at $0.90 RPS. Blushing Stick Emily Performance dropped from 1st to last as sample grew. The Performance LP format wins decisively at BOF but underperforms at MOF — funnel stage matters more than format.

Finding 03 · New
Funnel stage matters: TOF/MOF/BOF show different winners

Foundation Stick was the first launch to test the same LP formats across all three funnel stages, and the winners are not consistent:

TOF → Homepage leads ($0.79 RPS). MOF → FS Quiz leads ($1.28 RPS). BOF → Performance LP leads ($1.39 RPS). The implication: there may not be a single "best" LP for paid social — the answer depends on which funnel stage the ad creative targets.

Finding 04 · New
Quiz can win at MOF when product-integrated, but loses elsewhere

FS Quiz (with product=foundation-stick parameter) is the surprise winner at Foundation Stick MOF — beating Listicle by 16% and Homepage by 27% on RPS. But the same FS Quiz is in last place at Foundation Stick TOF ($0.48 RPS). Generic Quiz pages have lost as paid destinations elsewhere (Mini MB Quiz 3rd of 6). The MOF-specific win suggests product-integrated quizzes for warm audiences may have potential — worth a dedicated follow-up test.

Finding 05
Curated Collection pages can win during seasonal moments

TSNK Mother's Day Collection is the most extreme example: started 3rd at ~$1.47, ended 1st at $1.64 as the test ran through Mother's Day. Different from generic /collections/eyes type pages, which have always lost. The takeaway: a curated, seasonally-anchored Collection page can outperform Homepage during its moment — but this is a special case, not a default destination strategy.

Finding 06
Image-led Listicles beat Headline-led Listicles

ES Stick tested both and Image-led beat Headline-led by ~13% CVR (1.06% vs 0.94%). Foundation Stick MOF used the "5 Reasons Listicle" (Image-led) which finished 2nd at $1.10 RPS. Image-led layout remains the recommended Listicle format.

Finding 07
PDP, Shop All, and generic Collections still consistently underperform

PDP has finished last or near-last in every test where it's been included. Shop All has never finished better than 4th. Generic /collections/ paths (not seasonal/curated) have never won. Foundation Stick BOF ePDP tied with PDP and Homepage around $0.78, suggesting even enhanced PDP versions don't break the pattern.

Finding 08
TOF audiences are confirmed cold across launches

Blushing Stick TOF (3.6K visitors): CVR 0.08% across all variants. Foundation Stick TOF (15K visitors): CVR 0.59-1.03%. Both consistent with cold-traffic behavior — TOF tests need much higher volume to read winners than mid/bottom-funnel.

Open Questions

1. Why did Mini MB Homepage win when Lip Recharge and ES Stick Listicle LPs won?

Hypotheses: (a) MB is JRB's hero product so audience is pre-sold, needs less LP content; (b) Mini MB is a price-tier extension, not a new product, so Homepage browsing makes more sense. Foundation Stick is closer to ES Stick / Lip Recharge (new product, new use case) — which would argue for Listicle.

2. Does the Blushing Stick Branded "Homepage wins" finding actually hold at launch scale?

Blushing Stick has ~2K visitors per arm. ES Stick and Lip Recharge have 150K+. Volume transition may flip the winner. Worth re-testing during Foundation Stick launch with higher spend.

3. Image-led or Problem-First — which Listicle format for Foundation Stick?

Both have evidence. Recommendation: Image-led layout with Problem-First headline. Combine the winning elements from ES Stick (layout) and Lip Recharge (copy framing).

4. Should we test TOF separately for Foundation Stick?

Blushing Stick TOF was underpowered. If Foundation Stick launch gets meaningful TOF spend, rerun a TOF LP test at higher volume. Otherwise route TOF to the same Listicle LP.

Caveats to flag: Foundation Stick TOF, MOF, and BOF tests are all still running (started 6/2-6/3) — early reads may shift as samples grow. TSNK matured through Mother's Day, so the Mother's Day Collection win is partly a seasonal effect, not a generalizable pattern. The "Performance LP is our winning format" finding from earlier versions of this analysis has been revised — the data is now genuinely mixed across launches and funnel stages, with the format winning decisively at BOF but underperforming at MOF.
