Ranking Our Paid Social Destinations: What 7 Launch LP Tests Reveal

How custom LPs have performed against Homepage, PDP, Quiz, Shop All, and Collections on paid traffic · Blushing Stick, Lip Recharge, Mini Miracle Balm, Eyeshadow Stick

TL;DR

Across 7 tests and 4 launches, only two destinations have ever finished in the top 2 on RPS: custom Listicle LPs and Homepage. PDP, Quiz, Shop All, and Collections pages have never cracked the top 2 — and usually land in last place. Listicle LPs have won on our two biggest launches (Eyeshadow Stick, Lip Recharge, both 500K+ sessions). Homepage has won on our three smaller tests (Mini MB, Blushing Stick Branded, Blushing Stick Emily).

For Foundation Stick: Route paid social to a custom Listicle LP (Image-led, Problem-First copy) with Homepage as the fallback and control. Skip building dedicated Quiz, Collection, or Shop All destinations — the data says they're not worth the build.

Key Findings — Updated

Finding 01 · Revised
Listicle LPs beat Homepage at launch-scale volume, but not always

On our two biggest launches with 500K+ sessions (Eyeshadow Stick, Lip Recharge), Listicle LPs won by 12-22% CVR. On Mini MB (smaller scale) and Blushing Stick (early-stage), Homepage wins. The gap in launch volume matters — prospecting at scale rewards targeted LP content.

Finding 02
Image-led Listicles beat Headline-led Listicles

ES Stick tested both and Image-led beat Headline-led by ~13% CVR (1.06% vs 0.94%). For Foundation Stick launch, the Listicle variant should lead with a compelling before/after or product-in-use image, not a hook headline.

Finding 03
PDP, Collection, and Shop All pages lose every time

Across all 7 tests, these three LP formats never come in first and usually come in last. ES PDP hit a 0.44% CVR. Never default to these for paid social.

Finding 04
Quiz as a paid LP is not winning

MB Quiz came in 3rd of 6 on Mini MB. Blushing Stick Quiz had 0 orders. Quiz drives engagement but not purchase on cold/lukewarm paid traffic.

Finding 05
Social Proof framing underperformed Problem-First framing

Lip Recharge LP V2 (Social Proof Headline) was paused in favor of LP V1 (Problem First Headline). Worth considering for Foundation Stick LP copy: lead with a customer problem, not a social proof stat.

Finding 06
Storytelling-heavy LPs consistently underperform

Emily Storytelling came in 3rd on Blushing Stick Emily-Specific. Narrative content may be a conversion tax for warm paid traffic who are ready to shop. Exception: may work for YouTube (early signal, unconfirmed).

Open Questions

1. Why did Mini MB Homepage win when Lip Recharge and ES Stick Listicle LPs won?

Hypotheses: (a) MB is JRB's hero product so audience is pre-sold, needs less LP content; (b) Mini MB is a price-tier extension, not a new product, so Homepage browsing makes more sense. Foundation Stick is closer to ES Stick / Lip Recharge (new product, new use case) — which would argue for Listicle.

2. Does the Blushing Stick Branded "Homepage wins" finding actually hold at launch scale?

Blushing Stick has ~2K visitors per arm. ES Stick and Lip Recharge have 150K+. Volume transition may flip the winner. Worth re-testing during Foundation Stick launch with higher spend.

3. Image-led or Problem-First — which Listicle format for Foundation Stick?

Both have evidence. Recommendation: Image-led layout with Problem-First headline. Combine the winning elements from ES Stick (layout) and Lip Recharge (copy framing).

4. Should we test TOF separately for Foundation Stick?

Blushing Stick TOF was underpowered. If Foundation Stick launch gets meaningful TOF spend, rerun a TOF LP test at higher volume. Otherwise route TOF to the same Listicle LP.

Caveats: Launch volume is a meaningful confound in this analysis — Homepage winning on smaller tests may simply be a function of sample size, not audience. The clearer signal is that Listicles + Homepage are the only two formats that ever win, so building both is defensible regardless. Also: Blushing Stick tests are still running and Emily-Specific p2bb has trended from 100% to 61% as sample grew — worth a 5/1 check-in before final LP decisions are locked.
